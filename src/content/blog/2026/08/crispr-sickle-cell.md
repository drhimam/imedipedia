Sickle cell disease, a debilitating inherited blood disorder, has met its match with the historic approval and successful implementation of CRISPR-based gene editing.

## Mechanism of Action

The treatment, known as Casgevy, targets the BCL11A gene in autologous hematopoietic stem cells to reactivate fetal hemoglobin production, effectively preventing the sickling of red blood cells.
