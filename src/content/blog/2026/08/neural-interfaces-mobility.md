The boundary between mind and machine continues to blur as high-density neural interfaces enter clinical trials, restoring agency to individuals with severe spinal cord injuries.

## High-Density Recording

By utilizing micro-electrode arrays with thousands of recording sites, algorithms can translate neural activity patterns directly into complex movements, mapping motor commands in real-time.
