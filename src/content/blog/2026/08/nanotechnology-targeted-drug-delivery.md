Targeted chemotherapy has taken a major step forward with successful safety data emerging from phase I trials of polymeric nanoparticle delivery vehicles.

## The Mechanism of Selective Delivery
By coating chemotherapeutic agents in a specialized phospholipid bilayer decorated with cancer-specific ligands, the drug is delivered directly to tumor sites while bypassing healthy tissue. This significantly limits systemic side effects.

## Safety and Efficacy Profile
The trial verified that patients experienced negligible cardiotoxicity and neutropenia, which are common limiting factors in standard chemotherapy. Phase II trials will focus on clinical efficacy and tumor shrinkage metrics.
