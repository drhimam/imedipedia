Recent clinical trials have demonstrated that personalized mRNA vaccines can significantly reduce the risk of recurrence or death in patients with resected high-risk melanoma.

## Personalized Immunotherapy
The vaccine, mRNA-4157, is custom-tailored to each patient based on the unique mutational signature of their tumor. It targets up to 34 neoantigens, prompting a robust T-cell response designed specifically to eliminate micro-metastatic disease.

## Trial Design and Outcomes
Patients receiving the combination therapy of mRNA-4157 and pembrolizumab experienced a 44% reduction in the risk of recurrence or death compared to those receiving pembrolizumab monotherapy. These results represent a major milestone in oncology and point to a future of highly personalized cancer therapeutics.
